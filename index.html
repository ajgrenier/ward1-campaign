Where I Come From

Years ago I served as arena president; for the past 10 years my focus has been minor ball — I've been president of Seine River Minor Ball and have been serving as VP of Competition for Baseball Manitoba — plus lending a hand every year at the fall supper. None of that happens because of one person. Whatever success these programs have had comes from the coaches, board members, and volunteers who show up season after season; my part has been leading and organizing, but delegation and the people around me are what actually get things done.

Now, I want to shift that same attention to our community as a whole and the needs we're seeing rise. Small business needs, our aging population, and the influx of new development all require real attention. My goal is to support all of these areas, help with long-term community planning, and be there for the needs of our residents.

Why I'm Running

My career in construction has taken me from Head of Operations, to Project Manager, to Director — years spent managing budgets, building relationships with clients and subcontractors, and working with every level of government on permitting and approvals. That experience taught me how governance and process actually work, and how to listen to everyone at the table and find solutions that work for them.

01

Infrastructure & Project Oversight

Municipal governance spends millions on roads, utilities, drainage, and facilities. My background across operations, project management, and director-level roles means I understand bids, budgets, engineering realities, permitting, and how to hold contractors accountable for delivering quality work on time.

02

Fiscal Responsibility & Negotiation

Daily experience managing major contracts and dealing with clients and subcontractors translates directly into getting the best possible value for our tax dollars.

03

Practical Problem-Solving

I know how to bring different groups together — clients, crews, regulators, neighbours — understand what everyone needs, and find common ground that pushes projects across the finish line without unnecessary red tape.

Running for council isn't about politics for me — it's about service, accountability, and getting things done for our community.

Over the years, I've put my time where my heart is. Whether serving for years as arena president, president of Seine River Minor Ball, or helping out at the annual fall supper, I've always believed in rolling up my sleeves to support the facilities and events that bring our families together. I know our community because I've worked alongside you to build and maintain it.

Professionally, my career in construction and project leadership has given me the practical experience needed to make sound decisions at the council table.

I'm running with a simple commitment: transparent communication, practical decision-making, and keeping the best interests of our residents at the center of every vote. I'm here to represent our community with practical experience, steady leadership, and honest intentions.
